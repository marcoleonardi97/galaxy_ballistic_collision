# Ballistic collision of two galaxies

The project aims to simulate the penetration of a Milky Way-like galaxy by a smaller galaxy or cluster moving at ballistic velocities. The goal is to track how long it takes for the resulting "hole" to refill and to monitor the evolution of the density distribution over time. The simulation includes both stars and gas, but focuses solely on gravitational interactions without stellar evolution. Gravitational interactions will be handled by BHTree (or Ph4) and the hydrodynamics is modeled with Fi.
